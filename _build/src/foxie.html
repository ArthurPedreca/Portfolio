Foxie, an AI concierge for Flexiestays

Flexiestays runs serviced apartments in Bournemouth. Foxie lets guests ask about them in plain English and answers with things they can tap, not paragraphs.

Company
Flexiestays, UK
My role
Lead developer
Built with
Vercel AI SDK
Pattern
Tool calling and generative interface

How it answers

  1. The guest asks

    “Help me find accommodation”, typed or picked from a suggestion.

  2. Foxie calls a tool

    Instead of answering from memory, the model calls a tool that looks up the properties and their apartments.

  3. The answer arrives as interface

    The result streams into the chat as components: property cards with photos, guest and bedroom counts, and a button to explore each building.

  4. The next step is one tap away

    Suggestions like “Show me Tower House” or “Check availability” keep the conversation going.

Why cards

Someone choosing where to stay wants to see the place. A card with photos and capacity answers faster than a paragraph. And because the cards are filled by a tool call rather than written by the model, what the guest sees are the real listings.

Foxie also says plainly, under the input, that it is an AI and can make mistakes.

Around it

Foxie sits on the Nuxt booking website, next to the Flutter app and the internal dashboards I also run.
